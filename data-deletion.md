# Data Deletion Instructions

VideoSystem is a personal tool used only by its operator. It stores, on the operator's own computer, the access tokens and basic profile information of social media accounts the operator connected.

To delete this data:

1. Open VideoSystem → **Channels**, choose the account, and click **Disconnect**. Its tokens and profile information are deleted immediately.
2. Optionally revoke the app's access on the platform:
   - **Facebook:** Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations → remove "VideoSystem".
   - **YouTube / Google:** Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access → remove "VideoSystem".
   - **TikTok:** Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → remove "VideoSystem".
