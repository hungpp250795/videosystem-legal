# VideoSystem

**VideoSystem** is a personal, self-hosted tool that helps its operator, **HungPP**, turn short videos into Vietnamese versions and publish them to the operator's own channels on YouTube, Facebook (Page) and TikTok.

## What it does

1. **Import** — downloads a short video the operator is allowed to reuse, keeping a record of the original creator.
2. **Transcribe & translate** — creates subtitles with speech recognition and translates them into Vietnamese, all on the operator's own computer.
3. **Edit** — the operator reviews the subtitles, adjusts their style and position, and optionally adds a Vietnamese voice-over.
4. **Render** — produces the final video locally.
5. **Publish** — uploads the finished video to the operator's own accounts at a scheduled time, with the original creator credited and AI-generated content labeled as each platform requires.

## How it uses TikTok, YouTube and Facebook

The operator signs in with each platform's official login (OAuth). VideoSystem uses the granted permissions only to read basic account information and to upload videos that the operator prepared and approved, to the operator's own accounts. Tokens are stored encrypted on the operator's computer and are never shared. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy) and [Terms of Service](terms-of-service).

## Who can use it

VideoSystem is not a public service: it runs on one computer and is used only by its operator. There is no sign-up.
