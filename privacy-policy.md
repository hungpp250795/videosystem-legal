# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-25_

VideoSystem ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted tool operated by **HungPP** to edit short videos and publish them to the operator's own social media accounts (YouTube channel, Facebook Page, TikTok account). The app runs entirely on the operator's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## Information we access

When the operator connects a social media account, the app receives, through the platform's official OAuth login:

- basic profile information of the connected account (account/page/channel ID, name, avatar);
- access tokens (and refresh tokens where the platform provides them) that allow the app to upload videos and read basic statistics of the operator's own content.

The app requests only the permissions needed for these features: uploading videos, setting their title/description/privacy, and reading the publishing status.

## How information is used

Tokens and profile information are used solely to publish videos that the operator has prepared, at the time the operator chooses, and to show the publishing status inside the app. The app does not sell, share, or transfer this information to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

## Storage and security

All data is stored locally on the operator's computer in a database that is not exposed to the internet. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Nothing is sent to servers other than the platforms' own APIs.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account remains connected. Disconnecting an account in the app's "Channels" screen deletes its tokens and profile information immediately. You can also revoke access at any time from the platform's own settings (Google Account → Security → Third-party access; Facebook → Settings → Business Integrations; TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). See the [Data Deletion Instructions](data-deletion.md).
